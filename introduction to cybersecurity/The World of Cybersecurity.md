# The World of Cybersecurity

## What Is Cybersecurity?

- is an ongoing effort to protect individuals, organizations, and governments from digital attacks by protecting networked systems and data from unauthorized user or harm.

- Three Levels of Protection:
  - `Personal` – You need to safeguard your identity, data, and devices from cyber threats.
  - `Organizational` – Companies must protect their reputation, customer data, and internal systems.
  - `Government` – Protecting national security, economic stability, and citizen data is crucial in an increasingly digital world.

## Protecting Your Personal Data

- Personal data includes any information that can be used to identify you, both offline and online.
  - `Offline identity`
    - Your offline identity includes your real-life personal details, such as your name, age, and address. Identity thieves can steal this information through lost documents, mail fraud, or even by eavesdropping in public places.
  - `Online identity`
    - Your online identity includes usernames, social media profiles, and any personal details shared online. Limiting the personal information you expose can help protect you from identity theft and cybercrimes.

## Your Online Identity

- When creating an online username, avoid using personal details like your full name, birth year, or contact information. Consider these two secure choices:
  - jane.doe
  - jdoe.IT
  - jdoe1990
  - jdoe ✅
  - j.doe12 ✅

- Tips for Secure Usernames:
  - Avoid full names, addresses, or phone numbers.
  - Don’t use the same username and password combination.
  - Use different usernames for business and personal accounts.
  - Avoid patterns that can hint at your passwords.

## Your Data

- Personal data includes your name, Social Security number, driver's license, birthdate, and private messages. Cybercriminals exploit this information for identity theft, fraud, and impersonation.

- how hackers can get their hands on your personal data.
- 🔹 Medical Records
  - Your health data is stored in electronic records, making it vulnerable to breaches. Even fitness trackers collect sensitive medical data stored in the cloud.
- 🔹 Education Records
  - School records contain personal details, contact information, and even disciplinary reports.
- 🔹 Employment & Financial Records
  - Tax documents, paychecks, and bank statements hold valuable financial data that hackers can exploit.
